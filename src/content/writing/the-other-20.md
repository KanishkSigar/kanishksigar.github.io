most of what hurts is not deep.
it just hasn't been laughed at yet.

a bad week. a bad grade. a thing someone said
that you carried for days.
then you're at a table with your friends
and someone says something stupid
and it's gone.

80% of pain goes away with friends.

it's the other 20% i don't know what to do with.
