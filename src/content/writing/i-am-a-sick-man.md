"i am a sick man. i am a spiteful man. i am an unattractive man."

no writer opens like this. you don't get to.
you're supposed to earn the reader first.

but dostoevsky knew something:
a man who tells you the worst of it in the first line
is a man you will follow anywhere.

we've inverted this.
we introduce ourselves with the best of it now
and wonder why nobody follows.
