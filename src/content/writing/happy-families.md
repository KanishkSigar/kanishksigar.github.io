"all happy families are alike; each unhappy family is unhappy in its own way."

everyone quotes this. nobody notices tolstoy has it backwards,
and that he knew.

unhappiness is the boring one. it repeats.
the same silences at the same dinner table.
the same fight in a thousand houses on the same street tonight.
you could set your watch by it.

it's happiness that's strange and specific and impossible to copy.

he opens with a beautiful lie
and then writes eight hundred pages taking it apart.
most people never get past the first sentence.
