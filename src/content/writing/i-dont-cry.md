i'm not emotional. i don't cry when something bad happens, really bad.
i don't cry when someone dies.
i don't cry when someone leaves me.
i don't cry.

but i cry at the smallest things.
i cry at a scene in a movie, not even the big scene.
i cry when i read about love.
i cry when i see abandoned characters.

i cry my heart out, but i don't cry.
